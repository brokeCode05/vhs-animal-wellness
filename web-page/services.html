Our Services

Comprehensive veterinary care for your beloved pets

Vet Consultation

Professional advice and complete health check for your pets. Our experienced veterinarians provide thorough examinations and personalized care plans.

Canine & Feline Vaccinations

Complete vaccination programs for dogs and cats to protect them from common diseases. Timely immunizations following the latest veterinary guidelines.

Anti-rabies Vaccination

Protect your pets and family with rabies immunization. Required by law and essential for public health safety.

Heartworm Prevention

Prevent heartworm disease with proper medication and regular testing. Essential for your pet's long-term health.

Blood Tests (CBC, Blood Chem)

Accurate diagnostic laboratory testing to detect health issues early. CBC and Blood Chemistry panels identify infections and organ problems.

Pet Grooming & Supplies

Professional grooming services and essential pet products. Bathing, haircuts, nail trimming, ear cleaning — keeping your pets looking their best.

Spay & Neuter Services

Safe surgical procedures for population control. Reduces risk of certain cancers and helps reduce pet overpopulation.

Pet Microchipping

Permanent identification for your pets. A tiny microchip helps reunite lost pets with their owners through a nationwide scanning network.

Test Kits (Distemper, Parvovirus)

Rapid testing kits for common pet diseases. Quick and accurate results for timely treatment decisions.

Online Consultation

Virtual vet consultations for your convenience. Get expert advice from the comfort of your home via video call.

Home Service Visits

Veterinary care at your doorstep. Ideal for stressed, elderly, or multi-pet households.

Vet Health Certificates

Official health documentation for travel and compliance. Required for pet transport and international travel.
