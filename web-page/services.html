Our Services

Comprehensive veterinary care for your beloved pets

Vet Consultation

Professional advice and complete health check for your pets. Our experienced veterinarians provide thorough examinations and personalized care plans.

Canine & Feline Vaccinations

Complete vaccination programs for dogs and cats to protect them from common diseases and keep them healthy throughout their lives.

Pet Grooming and Pet Supplies

Professional grooming services and essential pet products to keep your furry friends looking and feeling their best.

Anti-rabies Vaccination

Protect your pets and family with rabies immunization. Required by law and essential for public health safety.

Pet Microchipping

Permanent identification solution for your pets. Helps reunite lost pets with their owners quickly and reliably.

Heartworm Prevention

Prevent heartworm disease with proper medication and regular testing. Essential for your pet's long-term health.

Blood Tests (CBC, Blood Chem)

Accurate diagnostic laboratory testing services to detect health issues early and guide treatment decisions.

Spay & Neuter Services

Safe surgical procedures for population control. Helps reduce pet overpopulation and provides health benefits.

Online Consultation

Virtual vet consultations for your convenience. Get expert advice from the comfort of your home.

Home Service Visits

Veterinary care provided in the comfort of your home. Ideal for pets that get stressed during travel.

Test Kits (Distemper, Parvovirus, etc.)

Rapid testing kits for common pet diseases. Quick and accurate results for timely treatment.

Vet Health Certificates

Official health documentation for travel and compliance. Required for pet transport and international travel.
